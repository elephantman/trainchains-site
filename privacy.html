Privacy Policy

Train Chains · Effective October 4, 2026

The short version

Train Chains does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your settings (sound, haptics, color-blind mode), stars, best scores and play statistics are saved locally on your device so the game can remember your progress. This information never leaves your device through the app, and deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the game sends your scores and achievements to Game Center so they appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; the developer does not receive your personal information. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Children

Train Chains is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
